Detailed description:
IRCTC Autofill by Karan saves you typing time when you book train tickets on irctc.co.in.

What it does:
• Fills your IRCTC user ID and password in the login popup. You type the captcha, and Sign In is pressed for you.
• Fills From/To stations, journey date, class and quota, then runs the search.
• Finds your train, selects your class and availability, and presses Book Now at the time you set (for example 10:00 AC / 11:00 non-AC Tatkal).
• Fills up to 6 passengers (name, age, gender, berth, food), mobile number and BHIM/UPI or card payment option.
• Shows a small on-screen status and retries automatically after common IRCTC errors.

What it does NOT do:
• It does not solve or bypass CAPTCHA or OTP.
• It does not make payments. You complete the payment yourself.
• It does not send your data anywhere. Everything stays in your own browser.

Privacy: No server, no tracking, no analytics. Data is stored only in Chrome's local storage on your computer.

Disclaimer: This is an independent tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to IRCTC or Indian Railways. Use it only for your own bookings and follow IRCTC's terms of use.
